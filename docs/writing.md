# Writing

I have been blogging for a long time, both personally and in a professional capacity. Blogs I worked on professionally fall into the following buckets:

- Authored: These are ones I directly authored or co-authored
- Editorial support: These are ones authored by others where I had editorial review

Early in my career, I wrote blogs directly. Later in my career, I shifted to helping others (often early in their careers/roles) to write compelling blogs as well as defining strategic blog content.
