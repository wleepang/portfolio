# Talks and video

Talks, webinars, workshops, and demos, newest first. I've probably given 100s of talks. These are the ones that I can remember and have publicly available records. Links are provided best effort.

Recordings are on YouTube; the playlist [Me @ AWS](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLc1ldzsdWv28) collects the videos I published while at AWS. Where only a recording exists, the date is when it was uploaded, which can be later than the event itself.

- **[A fast walk through Amazon Omics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1HHDyZwKaBA)** - *Demo, HPC Tech Shorts, uploaded Apr 2023.*

The page lists a recording.
- **[Integrate and derive insights from multi-modal health data (AIM206)](https://www.youtube.com/live/78vNPmkLFMI)** - *Session, re:Invent 2022 (recording uploaded Dec 2022), co-presenter with [Taha Kass-Hout](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tahak/).* A demo of how you can use all HealthAI services (HealthLake, HealthImaging, HealthOmics) in an integrative analysis journey across FHIR records, DICOM images, and genomic variants.
- **re:Invent 2022 HealthAI end-to-end** - *Breakout and builder session (clinical records, genomics, imaging), Nov-Dec 2022.*
